
# Runway Incursion Safety Risk Assessment Methodology

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes a preliminary approach for assessing safety risks associated with runway incursion scenarios.

The analysis is an independent educational project and is not an official airport SMS risk assessment.

## 2. Assessment Framework

The project follows the general FAA Safety Risk Management process:

1. Describe the operational system.
2. Identify hazards.
3. Analyze potential safety consequences.
4. Assess severity and likelihood.
5. Identify and evaluate risk controls.

## 3. Severity Categories

The following qualitative categories will be considered:

- Catastrophic
- Hazardous
- Major
- Minor
- Minimal

Definitions and applicability will be documented before ratings are assigned.

## 4. Likelihood Categories

Potential likelihood categories include:

- Frequent
- Probable
- Remote
- Extremely Remote
- Extremely Improbable

Likelihood assessments must consider the operational context, exposure, available evidence, and uncertainty.

## 5. Assessment Requirements

For each hazard scenario, the assessment will document:

- Hazard ID and operational context
- Potential safety consequence
- Possible contributing factors
- Existing and proposed controls
- Severity assessment and rationale
- Likelihood assessment and rationale
- Supporting evidence and assumptions
- Initial and residual risk, where justified
