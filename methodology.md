# Proposed Methodology

**Status:** Draft study plan; no completed empirical analysis is claimed.

## Research questions
1. What categories of hazards could contribute to runway incursions?
2. Which operational interfaces (ATC, flight crew, airport operations, vehicles) warrant closer examination?
3. What safety controls are described by publicly available aviation authorities, and what assumptions do those controls rely on?

## Boundaries
- U.S. civil airport surface operations, focused on protected runway areas.
- Use only publicly available material approved for reuse.
- Exclude confidential employer/client data, nonpublic safety reports, and personally identifying information.

## Process
1. Define operational context and relevant stakeholders.
2. Review FAA/NTSB definitions and published guidance.
3. Record candidate hazards and possible controls in the hazard register.
4. For each supported assertion, link to a primary source and note uncertainty.
5. If analyzing incidents, document dataset period, filters, missingness, definitions, and analytical methods.
6. Distinguish descriptive trends from causal claims.
7. Invite technical review before interpreting results or publishing recommendations.

## Quality safeguards
- Illustrative hazards are not presented as actual incident findings.
- Severity/likelihood scores will not be assigned without an explicit scoring rubric and defensible evidence.
- Recommendations will state assumptions and implementation responsibilities.
